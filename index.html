01 / fetch

Room snapshots

Each refresh pages the live room and merges it into the committed snapshot, so a record survives here even after the room drops it. Refreshes are run by hand, not on a timer — the date above is this page's real freshness, not a promise about the next one.

Scope of this record

This is an independent public archive, not an eligibility checker, and it is not affiliated with Flop Labs. Being listed means only that a matching room record was archived.

Private keys stay private

Never share or upload your private key or passphrase anywhere. Any site asking for it is a scam. A valid flow can ask you to sign with your own key; it never needs the key itself.
