Coverage

What this archive holds, and what it will never hold. The room is a ring buffer: records leave it permanently, and the read window returns only the newest 200. Anything evicted before the recorder reached it is gone from the public internet, and is drawn here as a gap rather than quietly omitted.

Flood measurement

Measured from this archive's own records, over the continuously captured window only. The question is simple: how much of the traffic is the same sentence arriving again, and how many identities post nothing else. This counts public text. It names no one, scores no one, and says nothing about anyone's eligibility for anything.

Continuous capture

A follower reads each room from its last known sequence and appends what it finds, so a record survives here after the room drops it. The room keeps only the newest 200 records and evicts the rest permanently, so whatever the recorder was not running for is gone from the public internet — and is written down as a gap rather than quietly skipped.

Scope of this record

This is a third-party public archive, not an eligibility checker, and it is not affiliated with Flop Labs. Being listed means only that a matching room record was archived.

Private keys stay private

Never share or upload your private key or passphrase anywhere. Any site asking for it is a scam. A valid flow can ask you to sign with your own key; it never needs the key itself.
